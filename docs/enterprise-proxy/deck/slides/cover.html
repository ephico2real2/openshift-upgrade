Threat model: updating our clusters through the proxy

The platform starts every update and calls named Red Hat hosts on TCP 443 through our proxy. Application pods have no way out.

2026-10-09 · proposed design

This deck is the threat model for the proxy approach: updating our OpenShift clusters by letting the platform reach Red Hat through our enterprise proxy. It covers the platform side only, and every control except our admission policy comes from Red Hat's OpenShift documentation. The platform owner decides on the proxy approach and takes the change to security on this threat model; the one condition is that applications cannot use the proxy by default.
