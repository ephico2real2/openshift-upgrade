The same update, step by step

  1. CVO checks for updates
  2. Admin picks the target
  3. CVO verifies the release
  4. CRI-O pulls the images
  5. CRI-O checks each signature (from 4.21)
  6. OLM installs approved operators; CRI-O pulls
  7. Application pods: blocked

9 / 12

Steps one to six are the platform's; every one crosses our proxy on TCP 443 to a named Red Hat host, with its controls marked on the arrow. Step seven shows that application pods never reach out: they are admitted only with allowed images and have no route. Step five exists from OpenShift 4.21 only; while the clusters are on 4.18 to 4.20 it is not active, and the CVO's release signature check in step three is the signature control.
