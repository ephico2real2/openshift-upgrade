SOLUTIONS.md, Recommendation · enterprise-proxy/THREAT-MODEL.md · 25

The SMEs and the Product Owner shape the direction of this platform offering; this is the SME's professional recommendation, from experience and modern best practice. The Ansible mirror is a lot of work to maintain, and that time is better spent on the update process itself, through the proxy. Ansible automates the upgrade in both cases: with the proxy approach the playbooks drive the retrofit and the update runs themselves; with the mirror approach they also have to run and maintain the mirror. What stays in every option becomes playbooks: the hops themselves, the update service, the registry configuration and catalog gating through GitOps. With the proxy enabled on 4.18 first, the hops to 4.20 and 4.22 run straight from Red Hat with no sync at all. The difference is the manual mirror, which the proxy approach eliminates. We are not looking for two-headed DNS but for a sustainable way to update as we grow; the disconnected setup came from OpenShift 3.x, where the proxy setup was a mess, and on 4.x the proxy is Red Hat's supported way for enterprise customers to take fixes as they ship, and keeps us ready for the growth that follows, OpenShift 5.x for example. The platform owner decides, and the decision is the proxy approach: the clusters take updates straight from Red Hat through the enterprise proxy, as a connected cluster does, with no mirror, no sync and no local Update Service. Other teams already update VMware and Tanzu Kubernetes Grid through the same proxy. The change goes to security on the threat model, and the one condition is that consumers and applications cannot use the proxy by default: the platform gives application pods no proxy settings (C2) and they have no route out (C1), proven by the runbook's tests 6 and 8 on every cluster. With that condition met, we are good. Fallback only, if the change is not approved: the mirror approach automated, the Ansible playbooks on a timer into the new registries. Red Hat Quay on-prem would only change the registry product of that mirror approach; it is not a third option. Under either option the clusters must be on 4.20 before 4.18's EUS term ends on 25 February 2027.
