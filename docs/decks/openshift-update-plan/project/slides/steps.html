Three steps, one decision

Step 1 · built

Ansible runs today's mirroring; we still copy everything

Step 2 · the decision

Clusters pull from Red Hat through the proxy; no sync

Step 3 · fallback only

Our own automated mirror, Ansible on a schedule; enterprise Quay is just the registry

Solid boxes are built; dashed boxes are proposed · 5

All three open the same Red Hat hosts at the firewall. They differ in which machines use them: one bastion per datacenter for Steps 1 and 3, every cluster node for Step 2. Step 2 is the platform owner's decision, taken to security on the threat model; Step 3 is the fallback only, and Red Hat Quay on-prem would only change its registry product. Step 1 runs the mirror until each cluster is retrofitted, and its playbooks run the retrofit and the hops under the proxy approach.
