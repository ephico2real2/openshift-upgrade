Only allowed registries: two layers

Nodes · every pull

image.config allowedRegistries (C5)

CRI-O refuses any other repository. Red Hat: "Registries not in your allowlist are blocked."

Admission · before a pod exists

Kyverno allowed registries (C8)

A pod naming any other registry is refused at oc apply. The list lives in a ConfigMap.

Allowed: Red Hat's registries and our internal registries. Nothing else.

Red Hat: images-configuration-allowed.adoc · C8 is our policy · 8

The nodes enforce the allowlist on every pull, including builds and static pods. The admission policy stops a bad image earlier, with a clear error, before a pod is created. We use Kyverno's standard allowed-registries policy; another policy engine, such as the built-in ValidatingAdmissionPolicy, could do the same. The internal registry names in the list are illustrations: the enterprise registry, whether Artifactory, a GitLab container registry, Harbor or Quay, and the oc-mirror registry until it is retired.
