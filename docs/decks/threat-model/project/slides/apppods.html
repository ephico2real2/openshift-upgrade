Proof: tests 6 and 8, a test pod gets no proxy settings and cannot go out directly · the one condition for security · 7

Three independent controls: the platform's proxy setting never reaches application pods, the firewall gives them no route, and the admission policy keeps their images to the allowed registries. A test pod on a live cluster proves the first two: it has no proxy settings and cannot reach the internet directly. This is the one condition of the change the platform owner takes to security. A team that sets a proxy in its own deployment does so by choice, under our existing policy; that is not a platform gap.
